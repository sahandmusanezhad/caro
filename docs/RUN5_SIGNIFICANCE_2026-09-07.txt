WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SETTLE 
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ✗ the interval excludes zero — the model being WORSE is not distinguishable from noise
  ✗ the interval excludes the gate's 1.10 threshold — 'worse by more than 10%' is NOT established

  The REJECT does not survive its own uncertainty. On the
  registered criterion the gate rejected; on the evidence the
  difference is not distinguishable from sampling noise, and
  UNJUDGEABLE would be the more honest verdict.

  Either way this is a finding about the GATE, not about Run 5's
  execution. `HierarchicalGate.mae_tolerance` is a bare comparison
  on a point estimate, while the calibration criterion beside it
  requires 2.5 SE — D33 fixed winner's curse on one field and left
  the other one raw. Changing it now would be tuning after a loss,
  so it is recorded and left alone; the next registration decides.
