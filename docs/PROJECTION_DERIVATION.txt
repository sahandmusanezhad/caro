======================================================================
READS NOTHING CAN SUPPLY
======================================================================

  A consumer reads these; no FetchOutcome field carries them, so
  they are None on every row and always will be:

    fuel                    PARSED, then dropped at this boundary
    gearbox                 PARSED, then dropped at this boundary
    price_currency_raw      PARSED, then dropped at this boundary

  For the ones marked PARSED: the parser produces a value, the
  corpus reader asks for it, and FetchOutcome has nowhere to put
  it in between. That is D51 — a value is not collected until it
  survives to where it is read.
