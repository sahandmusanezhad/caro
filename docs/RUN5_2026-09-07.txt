WHY THE ESTIMATOR LOST, AND WHY THAT IS NOT A BUG
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Run 3's corpus was four Saipa models. Run 5's spans 44 makes:

      min     350,000,000
      median  2,600,000,000
      max     50,000,000,000        max/min = 143x

  Partial pooling shrinks a thin trim's estimate toward its parent and, where
  the parent is itself thin, toward the global level. Across a 143x price
  range that shrinkage is not a mild correction, it is a large error, and the
  parent-median baseline — which never pools past `make|model` — is simply
  better behaved on this corpus.

  So the result is real and the mechanism is legible. What it is NOT is a
  finding that partial pooling is the wrong estimator for Iranian used cars.
  It is a finding about THIS corpus, whose composition my acquisition chose.


THE PRE-REGISTRATION'S OWN GAP
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  This is the part worth keeping. RUN5_SPEC.md §2 constrained the trim-size
  distribution in detail — counts, floors, slice sizes — and said NOTHING
  about price heterogeneity or make composition. Both corpora satisfy the
  registered shape; they are not comparable estimation problems.

  Nothing was violated. The registration was simply blind to the variable
  that turned out to dominate the outcome, and it was blind to it in advance,
  which is the only way that fact could have been established.

  A REJECT obtained on a corpus the registration did not constrain is a valid
  result about the registered question and a weak one about the interesting
  question. Both halves of that sentence have to survive into whatever is
  written next.


WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN NEXT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Not: retune the estimator until it beats the baseline on this corpus.
  Not: re-run on a narrower corpus and report the better number.
  Not: describe this as "partial pooling does not work".

  D35 is unchanged and now applies in the other direction: acquisition and
  estimator do not move in the same run. A comparison stratified by price
  band, or a corpus registered for homogeneity as well as shape, is a NEW
  experiment with its own registration — and it has to say which question it
  is answering, because it is not this one.

  D12 already covers the shipping decision: if the baseline wins, ship the
  baseline and say so. On this corpus, the baseline wins.
